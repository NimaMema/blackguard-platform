    04 / THE REALM

    Device inventory.

    Devices known from approved agents, router DHCP leases, a collector's passive ARP cache, or your own entries. Each attribute is labelled confirmed, reported or inferred. BLACKGUARD never scans your network.

    —
    05 / THE GARRISON

    Agents.

    Each collector has its own credential, is approved by an administrator after a fingerprint check, and can be rotated or revoked at any time. Agents buffer events locally while the server is unreachable.

    ENROLLMENT CODES
    06 / GUARDIAN RESPONSE

    Measured response.

    Actions are proposed from an incident, approved by an administrator who acknowledges the impact, carried out by a person, and recorded with a rollback plan. BLACKGUARD does not block, disconnect, reset or counter-attack anything by itself.

    —
    07 / THE CODEX

    Rules of engagement.

    Deterministic, documented rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK where defensible. Tune thresholds to your household; every change is audited.

    08 / THE CHRONICLE

    Audit log.

    Sign-ins, approvals, rule changes, exports and every response decision. Entries are hash-chained so tampering with history is detectable.
